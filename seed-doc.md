# The problem

Student-athletes need to manage their time because they have to balance sports and school.

# Who has it

Student-athletes who need to balance their schoolwork and sports.

# What my app does today

ClearTime helps student-athletes manage their time. It uses AI to make suggestions and allows users to add and remove events from a calendar. The wording of the AI's suggestions was changed to make them more user-friendly.

# What testers told me

- **Nate:** Wanted some of the AI suggestions reworded to make them more user-friendly.
- **Kolby:** Wanted the ability to add events to the calendar because the app would be unusable without it.
- **Adrian:** Wanted the ability to remove events so users could fix mistakes.

# What people use now instead

Google calender, Apple calender, Apple reminder, Physical planner

# What I don't know yet

If people will actually use the app and not just say they would.
